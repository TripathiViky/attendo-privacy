Privacy Policy

Attendo for Android · Last updated 10 November 2026

Attendo does not collect, transmit, or share any data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Attendo (“the app”) is published by Vikas Tripathi.

What the app stores

The app stores the following on your device only:

  • Employee records you create (name, employee ID, department, position, level, join date, work schedule)
  • Rooms you create (name, type, capacity)
  • Attendance records (check-in time, check-out time, room, notes)
  • Your app preferences (which profile is yours, which levels are visible)

This data is written to the app’s private storage area on your device. On Android, this area is sandboxed by the operating system: other apps cannot read it.

What the app does not do

  • The app does not have internet access. Its Android manifest does not request the INTERNET permission, so the operating system blocks any network connection the app could attempt.
  • The app requests no Android permissions at all — no camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or advertising ID.
  • The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No data is ever sent to the developer or to any third party.

Sharing data yourself

The History screen has an export button that builds a CSV of the selected day and opens your device’s standard share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app to send it to. Once you share the file, that destination app’s own privacy policy applies.

Deleting your data

  • Profile → Data & Privacy → Delete all data removes every employee, room, and attendance record from the device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its stored data.

Because nothing is uploaded, there is no server-side copy for us to delete.

Children

Attendo is a workplace tool and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data of any kind, from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL and the “Last updated” date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the app in general, can be raised at github.com/TripathiViky/attendo-privacy/issues.
